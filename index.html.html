Our Blueprint for India

The Manifesto

Not empty slogans. Real, actionable reforms grounded in accountability, transparency, and the dignity of every Indian citizen.

01
🔒

Leak-Free Examination System

Every paper leak is a crime against India's youth. We will build an impenetrable exam system using technology, accountability, and strict law.

  • Independent National Exam Commission with no political interference
  • End-to-end encrypted digital question paper delivery
  • 10-year non-bailable sentence for leaking exam papers
  • Whistleblower protection and rewards for exposing leaks
  • Live CCTV and AI monitoring at all exam centres
  • Automatic re-exam with full compensation in case of breach
02
⚖️

Zero Corruption Framework

Corruption is not just illegal — it is immoral. We will create systems where honest work is rewarded and dishonesty has nowhere to hide.

  • Fast-track corruption courts — verdict within 1 year
  • 100% digitisation of government contracts and tenders
  • Mandatory public disclosure of all assets by officials
  • Anonymous digital complaint portal with real follow-up
  • Third-party audits for all government schemes
  • Lokpal with teeth — real investigative & prosecutorial power
03
📚

World-Class Public Education

Every Indian child deserves a school with clean toilets, trained teachers, electricity, and internet — regardless of their PIN code or caste.

  • All government schools upgraded to minimum standards in 3 years
  • Teacher hiring on merit only — no political appointments
  • Free internet & laptop for every student from Class 9
  • Dual-language curriculum (Hindi + English) in all schools
  • IIT/NIT-level coaching integrated into government colleges
  • Biometric teacher attendance with salary linked to performance
04
🏥

Universal Dignified Healthcare

No Indian should die because they cannot afford treatment. Healthcare is a right — not a privilege for the rich.

  • 1 fully functional primary health centre per 5,000 population
  • All essential medicines free at government hospitals
  • Doctor and nurse vacancies filled within 6 months of taking power
  • Mental health as a core part of public health policy
  • 24×7 telemedicine for rural areas
  • Strict penalties for hospitals overcharging or denying treatment
05
🌿

Clean, Liveable Cities & Villages

Clean streets are not a luxury. Citizens pay taxes and deserve basic sanitation, functional drains, and uncontaminated water.

  • Door-to-door segregated waste collection in all urban areas
  • Strict penalties for littering and illegal dumping — no exceptions
  • 100% tap water coverage with quality testing by 2028
  • Green cover mandate: 20% of every new development as green space
  • Transparent "Swachh" rankings for every local body
  • Public toilet revolution: clean, safe, 24×7 accessible
06
🚫

No Freebies. Only Opportunity.

We believe in investing in people's capability — not buying their votes. Every rupee of public money must create lasting value.

  • Zero cash handouts for vote-bank politics
  • Skill-linked grants: earn while you learn programmes
  • Subsidies only for genuinely below-poverty-line citizens
  • Fiscal responsibility act: no populist spending without approval
  • MSME loans & self-employment support instead of handouts
  • Transparent public budget — every citizen can track spending

100-Day Action Plan

What We Will Do First

If voted to power, these are our non-negotiable first steps. We will be held accountable — publicly, every single day.

01

Declare Exam Leaks a National Emergency

On Day 1, a Presidential Order will classify exam leaks as a crime against the state — equivalent to sedition. A dedicated Special Investigation Team will be set up, with a 90-day deadline to arrest and charge all perpetrators of leaks from the past 10 years.

🔴 Non-Negotiable
02

Launch Anti-Corruption Digital Infrastructure

Within 30 days, we will roll out a real-time government spending dashboard visible to every citizen. Every contract above ₹1 lakh will require three competing bids, digital signatures, and geo-tagged photo evidence of work done.

🟠 30 Days
03

Fill Every Teacher & Doctor Vacancy

India has over 10 lakh vacant teaching posts and 80,000 vacant doctor posts in government service. We will initiate a national drive to fill all vacancies within our first 6 months, using merit-only selection with no political interference.

🟢 6 Months
04

Introduce the No-Freebie Fiscal Code

We will introduce legislation requiring any scheme distributing goods or cash to pass an independent fiscal sustainability test. No political promise can bypass this process. The code will be publicly debated, passed in Parliament, and impossible to repeal without a two-thirds majority.

🔵 Policy Reform
05

Create 50 Lakh Skilled Jobs in 2 Years

Not on paper — real, verifiable, private-sector backed employment through a national skill-to-job pipeline. Partner with industries, map local demand, train youth accordingly, and provide verified placement — not just training certificates that gather dust.

🟠 2 Years
